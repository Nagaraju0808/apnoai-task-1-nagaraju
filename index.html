About This Project

The Student Attendance Management System is designed to replace manual paper registers with an efficient digital workflow in schools and colleges.

It eliminates proxy attendance, saves teachers' classroom time, and automatically calculates attendance percentages for academic eligibility.

Key Features

Real-Time Tracking

Quickly mark and log student attendance per subject and period with instantaneous cloud synchronization.

Automated Reports

Generate detailed monthly records, defaulter lists, and analytical insights with a single click.

Parent Alerts

Send automated notifications and SMS updates to parents whenever a student is absent or late.
